Long-form input — briefing notes, feedback on a piece, campaign description.

```jsx
<TextArea label="Briefing" rows={6} placeholder="Objetivo, público, referências…" />
<TextArea label="Comentário" help="A equipe do cliente recebe por e-mail" />
```

Vertically resizable only. Keep `rows` at 4 unless the field is the point of the screen.
